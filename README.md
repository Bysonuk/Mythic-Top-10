# Mythic Top 10

**What are the best players in the world actually doing?**

Warcraft Logs knows. It has the rankings for every boss and every spec, and
inside each one sit the details that matter: the stat spread, the trinkets, the
talent build. The trouble is getting at them. Checking one spec on one boss means
opening a ranking, picking a log, finding the player, opening their gear, then
their talents, then doing it again for the next player, and again for the next
boss.

Mythic Top 10 does that reading for you, every day, for every spec, and puts the
answers in one place.

---

## What it tells you

For each of the ~40 specs, on every Mythic boss of the current raid, it pulls the
top 10 logs and reads out of them:

- **Secondary stats at the pull** — Critical Strike, Haste, Mastery and
  Versatility, as ratings and as a share of the total
- **Both trinkets**, with item levels
- **The combat potion** they drank
- **The talent build**, as an import code you can paste straight into the game
- Output, item level, guild, realm, kill time, and a link back to the log

---

## Why it's useful

**It answers the questions sims can't.** A sim tells you what's optimal on paper.
This tells you what ten people who beat the boss actually brought, which is a
different and often more honest answer.

**It shows the spread, not just the winner.** Seeing that nine of the top ten
stack haste is a much stronger signal than seeing the top parse do it. Where the
top ten disagree, that's worth knowing too.

**It's quick to compare.** One grid shows every spec's stat split side by side.
Useful for your own class, more useful when you're deciding what to play or
helping someone else gear.

**Nothing to interpret.** The talent build is a code you paste. The trinkets are
named. The potion is named.

---

## Two ways to read it

**The website** gives you the full picture: a grid comparing every spec, then a
page per spec with the stat split, the range each stat covers across the top ten,
trinket usage, talent builds grouped by popularity, and a card for every player.

<https://bysonuk.github.io/Mythic-Top-10/>

**The addon** puts the same data in game, on `/ms`. It opens on the spec you're
playing, and every player has a button that hands you their talent code, ready
for the game's Import box. Handy at a dummy, or mid-raid when someone asks what
the top logs are running.

---

## How current is it?

It refreshes every night. New entries into a top ten are picked up the same way,
so what you're reading is this week's rankings rather than something captured at
patch launch and left to rot. The site shows how long ago it last updated.

---

## Fair warning

Top logs come from the best raiders in the world, in well-organised groups, with
the best gear in the game. Their stat spread is the product of the items they got
rather than a target to chase, and the fastest kill isn't always the most
survivable one. Read it as evidence of what works at the top, not as a
prescription for your own run.
